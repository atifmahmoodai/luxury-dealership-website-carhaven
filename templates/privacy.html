Enquiries

The form records your name, email, optional phone, message, selected vehicle and consent to be contacted about that enquiry. Authorized staff use these details to respond and record follow-up notes. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Site operation

Necessary security cookies protect forms and staff sessions. A keyed hash of the connecting IP address supports short-window abuse limits. This build does not include advertising pixels or third-party analytics.

Access and retention

Enquiries remain in the dealership's database and may appear in secured backups. The operator must define retention and deletion schedules. Staff can delete enquiry records; backup expiry follows the operator's retention policy.
