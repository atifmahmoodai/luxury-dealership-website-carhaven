# Luxury Dealership Website (CarHaven)

This repo is a project brief, not the client's private code. It records what the buyer asked for so the build can be scoped against a real paid post.

## What this repo is about

CarHaven, a luxury car sales business, posted for a website with inventory pages and a backend staff can update. Budget was $15–$30/hour, 1–3 months, open to proposals.

## Source post

https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Website-Developer-Needed-for-Luxury-Car-Dealership-Website_~022033603080046612884/

## Client requirements

- Luxury look, mobile, fast, SEO-friendly.
- Inventory list, vehicle detail pages with photos and specs, contact form, WhatsApp or phone, About page.
- Staff must be able to add and remove cars without a developer.
- WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or custom. Inventory-system experience is a plus.
- They provide logo, photos, and company info.
- Slogan on the post: Drive Away With Confidence.

## Notes

- Requirements are taken from the public job post. They are not a signed contract.
- Posts can close or change. Check the source link before bidding.
- This repository was created as a working brief for atifmahmoodai.
